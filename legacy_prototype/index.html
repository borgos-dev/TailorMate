🎓 Database Normalization Rationale (1NF, 2NF, 3NF)

Use these precise academic definitions and project examples when answering examiner questions:

1NF (First Normal Form)

Rule: Each field must contain atomic (indivisible) values with no repeating groups.
Project Implementation: Clothing items are not stored as a single comma-separated text string ("Shirt, Trouser"). Instead, each individual garment has its own atomic row in order_items.

2NF (Second Normal Form)

Rule: Must be in 1NF and have no partial dependencies on composite primary keys.
Project Implementation: Item quantity and unit price depend on the specific item, but customer details depend on the customer. Customer information is kept in customers and referenced via foreign keys.

3NF (Third Normal Form)

Rule: Must be in 2NF and have no transitive dependencies (non-key fields must not depend on other non-key fields).
Project Implementation: Staff names and roles depend on staff_id, not on an order. Therefore, staff details are stored in staff, and the Many-to-Many assignment is decoupled into the order_staff junction table.

💡 Presentation Flow & Defense Q&A Cheat Sheet

Q: Why separate payments from orders?

Answer: Work progress (physical tailoring) and financial transactions are completely different business lifecycles. A customer can have an order that is In Progress while being Partially Paid, or an order that is Ready while still having an outstanding balance.

Q: Why is an order_staff junction table necessary?

Answer: Orders and Staff share a Many-to-Many (M:N) relationship. One complex agbada or suit may require a cutting master, an assembly stitcher, and an embroidery specialist. Similarly, one tailor works on multiple orders concurrently.

Q: How does the system solve the tailor deadline problem?

Answer: By continuously monitoring the difference between the agreed collection date and the current date (DATEDIFF(due_date, CURDATE())) for any order whose status is not yet Ready or Collected. This enables proactive communication before the customer travels to the shop.
